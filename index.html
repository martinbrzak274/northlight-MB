Frequently asked questions

How big is Northlight Studio?

We are 25 people: designers, strategists and developers. Small teams of three to five work on each project, so you always know who to talk to.

How long does a project take?

A brand identity usually takes six to ten weeks. A website or app design takes eight to twelve weeks. We agree a schedule with you before we start.

Do you work with small companies and start-ups?

Yes. About half of our clients are small companies. We can start with a short workshop and grow the project step by step.

How do I start a project?
