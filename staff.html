Staff accounts

This is a learning model, not a real security system.
  • All people, passwords and codes are fake. Do not enter real credentials.
  • Everything runs in your browser and is saved in localStorage, where anyone with access to this browser can read or edit it.
  • The "authenticator" below stands in for a phone app. In real life the secret lives only on the phone and the server.
  • Real systems check passwords and codes on a server, rate-limit attempts and log activity. None of that is modelled here.
